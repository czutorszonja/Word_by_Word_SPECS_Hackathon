# Development timeline: Word by Word

Screenshots taken during the build, for the project's development timeline.
They show how the café Lens changed from first stand-in shapes to the finished scene.

File names: `<date>_<time>_<step>_<what it shows>.png`, so they sort in build order.

**Visual timeline:** [`../timeline/index.html`](../timeline/index.html) shows the build stage by stage (18–25 Nov; the page numbers the stages 1–18 in order and shows the three outside trials, stages 18–20 here, as one card; the file names keep their original numbers), one card per stage with what changed and its best screenshots (63, cropped to the Specs view); open it in a browser and click a picture to enlarge it.

|---|---|
| 01 | Blocky first build: the café made of simple boxes and cylinders (stand-in shapes) |
| 02 | Basic flow working: setup panel, toast, Learn, Use it live, Practice |
| 03 | Realistic rebuild: generated 3D props, textured room, realistic server |
| 04 | LEAF test runs |

| 06 | Phase A: first mode colours and the new sandwich ingredients |
| 07 | Phase B: bigger café, realistic and varied people, black "Plain Studio" preview backdrop, cut line fixed, soft mode colours |
| 08 | Phase D: the story tour following the learner's storyboard (arrival → point to reveal → listen → save → Use it live → reuse the phrase → order → table 8 → practice). The 14:04 set is the first run; the 14:14 set is after moving the hover label above the tray |
| 09 | Re-layout from the Lens Studio AI's proposal: the coffee bar service-only, a window bar for seating |
| 12 | Clean-up after a visual audit: every ingredient in exactly one container (no pan inside a pan), nothing floating, the clear pastry case with the cake under a dome, the coffee station on the same counter, the entrance (NYITVA outside, ZÁRVA inside) and the wall menu board |

| 16 | Full walkaround after the 2026-11-21 feedback: five real seated guests (two friends chatting at table 10), the barista in a canvas apron, hand-lettered chalk menu (Kalam), bigger phrase boxes and cards, the saved-words list with a listen icon on every word, the Menu chip moved out of the way |
| 17 | 22 Nov: the younger guests seated (F07 + F08 chatting at table 10, M09 at table 5) and the hidden old people deleted; the first whole-café set — dollhouse overviews with the ceiling hidden, the four inside corners and every walkaround spot; the street windows now show the café inside instead of black glass; a gallery of three framed prints and two shelves on the bare wall behind tables 8–12. The `_17_tour_` frames are the story tour after the placement fixes: the barista's caption as a subtitle under his face (off the menu board), the Menu button low-left (with a Use it live chip that rises above the phrase), the practice board at 65 cm |
| 18 | 24 Nov: one coherent outside. Every window had shown a different city view and season (bare autumn trees, a summer park, the Danube from high up, photos with their own window frames). Now the window-bar side looks onto one leafy square and the door side onto one tram street, all on the same sunny late-spring morning, each window from its own angle; the view through the door is the same street at a believable scale. Replaced the same day by stage 19 |
| 19 | 24 Nov: one real street outside. The learner found the six generated photos still unrelated (and the tram seemed to drive into the café). A real 360° street panorama (Poly Haven "Zawiszy Czarnego", CC0) now wraps around the café, and the windows are real openings, so every window and the door show the same street corner from their own position and the view shifts as you move; outside, the start point stands in that street instead of a black void. Replaced the same day: the street was too dark and brick-heavy for the warm café |
| 20 | 24 Nov: a sunny square outside. Same panorama technique with Poly Haven "Piazza Bologni" (Palermo, CC0), slightly brightened: the window-bar windows look across the square to an olive tree and warm stone facades, the door faces a monument and a café terrace, and the start point outside stands beside that terrace. Replaced the same day: it still read as a photo pasted around a 3D room |
| 21 | 24 Nov: a simple street of our own. No photo: a small 3D street in the café's style (cream and ochre houses, limestone pavement, road, parked car, a calm lawn, a soft blue sky gradient). Outside, before going in, the learner can look left (tram stop, traffic light, bicycle: Street words), right (park with bench, flower bed, fountain, trees: Park words) and behind (POSTA, GYÓGYSZERTÁR, BOLT across the road). The windows show the same street and park; DC6 passes |
| 21 (25 Nov) | The street and park things became words: before going in, with no mode picked yet, pointing at the tram stop, traffic light, bicycle, car, the three shops, the bench, flower bed, fountain, a tree, the road, pavement, lawn or path names a word saved on an earlier outing (Street / Park) and a pinch says it; café words stay quiet until a mode is picked. In Learn their card shows "Saved ✓" and has no Use it live link. New test DC7_OutsideWords; DC2, DC3, DC6, DC7 pass |
| 22 | 25 Nov: calmer cards (learner: less on each card, easier to read). Word card: word (larger), meaning, close, Listen / Save / Use it live; the status sentence became the Listen button's "Playing…" and the mode chip moved to the Menu chip, which now names the mode in every mode. Phrase panel: no chip or "SAY IT AT THE COUNTER" heading, larger phrase and English. Hover labels about 20 % larger with a bigger English line; their live buttons 35 % larger. "Say it out loud" larger, its longest lines shortened. The table question's caption only translates; "Turn around and tap an empty table's number" is a hint above the server. Saved words: bold word, meaning, and the date only when sorted by date. DC1–DC7 and the story tour pass; before/after shots |
| 23 | 26 Nov: after the learner tried it. Saved words one step at a time (By category / By date added / A to Z → categories A–Z or time ranges → words, Back steps up); Practice opens in view and always has a set; outside words reachable by pointing (25 m ray); Use it live as real-life help (no Ready to order, the server notices you, "You could say" replies, the table number you look at fills "A … asztalnál ülök.", no hint / arrow / chime). Evening: DC1–DC7 and the story tour pass in the preview; the server now notices you only when you look up at him. 27 Nov: the `_23_saved-words_` shots walk through the Menu and the saved words step by step; the `_23_real-life-help_` shots show an outside label in Learn, "You could say" on the counter, the table question, "A … asztalnál ülök." with its blank, table 8 read by looking, the filled reply and Practice opening in front. Taken after a review against the Specs UI sizes: the Menu chip kept inside the view, the saved words at 80 cm with a margin, Practice following a downward gaze. DC1–DC7 and the tour pass |
| 24 | 27 Nov: Word by Word works for any language pair; Hungarian is the demo's language (learner). The `_24_languages_` shots: the setup panel's note ("Change them any time in the menu: Languages"), the menu with Words saved and Languages in one row, the Languages home (I speak English, I'm learning Hungarian · Magyar), the I'm-learning and I-speak lists, and "Deutsch is not in this demo yet". The `_24_demo-video_` shots are frames of DEMO_Video, the hands-free demo run (172 s): the tram stop word and the suggestion outside, walking in, the sentence ready at the counter, table 8 read by a glance, Practice seated at table 8, the Deutsch note. DC1–DC7, the story tour and DEMO_Video pass |
