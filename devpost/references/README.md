## Coffee station and pastry inspiration (sent 2026-11-19, described here because the images could not be saved from the chat)

- **Wood counter café:** one continuous counter of warm vertical wood panels with a brushed-steel top. The espresso machine and grinders sit at the end of the same counter by the window, a wooden glass-fronted pastry case with price labels stands on the counter, a black-and-white checker floor, opal globe pendants, open wooden shelves with small objects and plants, tall windows onto the street.
- **Red tile coffee bar:** a deep red glossy tile counter front with a light wooden top; two coffee grinders with clear bean hoppers, the espresso machine behind them, tall stacks of takeaway cups, lots of potted plants on the counter, framed prints on the wall, red cage pendant lights with Edison bulbs.
- **Mosaic counter with glass pastry case:** a curved counter with a blue-and-white mosaic front; on top, a clear multi-tier glass pastry case where every pastry is visible; the espresso machine on the back counter right behind it with cups on top; long wooden shelves with plates, teapots and cups along the back wall; a wooden ceiling with track lights.

**Takeaways for Word by Word:** the coffee station is part of the same counter run as the sandwich and pastry areas (no gap, same materials); grinders, milk (oat, almond, cow's), cups and plants sit on the counter; the pastry case glass is clear so the pastries can be seen; baked goods are covered.
